\sceneheading{INT./EXT. OBSERVATORY, WORKING SESSION - DAY}
\action{Biruni and Pandit, working the same eclipse prediction in parallel, each on his own instrument, in his own notation. Neither explains his method to the other. Neither can read the other's page.}
\action{They finish within moments of each other. Compare results --- not the working, just the final numbers.}
\action{The numbers agree.}
\action{Neither man says anything for a moment. It isn't triumph. It's closer to recognition --- two people who have just learned, without yet being able to say why, that they are doing the same thing.}
\action{Biruni's notebook page, glimpsed close: the layout deliberately echoes the astrolabe-correction pages from Khwarezm --- same hand, same habits, now describing an instrument he'd never seen a month ago.}

\sceneheading{INT. STUDY - DAY}
\action{The central scene of the act.}
\action{Biruni has been working through a term --- PRAMĀṆA --- that keeps appearing in Pandit's explanations of how a claim becomes knowledge rather than mere assertion. He proposes an Arabic gloss for it, something in the region of "proof" or "evidence."}
\charcue{BIRUNI}
\begin{dialogue}
If I write it as "proof" ---\\
\end{dialogue}
\charcue{PANDIT}
\begin{dialogue}
Then you've written a court document. Not what I mean.\\
\end{dialogue}
\charcue{BIRUNI}
\begin{dialogue}
"A means of valid knowledge," then. Closer?\\
\end{dialogue}
\charcue{PANDIT}
\begin{dialogue}
Closer. Still not it. Your word already has a job. It works for a judge, weighing one claim against another after the fact. Mine works before that --- it's what lets a claim be the kind of thing that could be weighed at all.\\
\end{dialogue}
\charcue{BIRUNI}
\begin{dialogue}
Then it isn't a word. It's a category.\\
\end{dialogue}
\charcue{PANDIT}
\begin{dialogue}
It's several categories, and how many of them count depends who you ask inside my own tradition. Perception counts. Inference counts. Some schools count testimony. Some don't. You're not translating a word. You're translating an argument that hasn't finished happening.\\
\end{dialogue}
\action{Biruni sits with this. Doesn't immediately write anything. This is new --- earlier in the film, the instinct to resolve into a clean note would have been immediate.}
\charcue{BIRUNI}
\begin{dialogue}
Then I won't translate it.\\
\end{dialogue}
\charcue{PANDIT}
\begin{dialogue}
You have to write something.\\
\end{dialogue}
\charcue{BIRUNI}
\begin{dialogue}
I'll write what it isn't. And why.\\
\end{dialogue}
\action{He picks up the stylus. On the notebook page --- held on camera --- he does not write a translated term. He writes, instead, a description of the gap itself: what "proof" captures, what it misses, why no single Arabic word can hold both.}
\action{This is the notebook's first entry of this kind in the film: not a record of an equivalence, but a record of a failure to find one, made precise enough to be useful anyway.}
\transition{CUT TO:}
\sceneheading{INT. STUDY - NIGHT}
\action{Biruni alone, re-reading the page from the day. Old habit surfaces --- he begins, almost without noticing, to compress his own note back down into something cleaner, more citable.}
\action{He stops. Reads what he's just written. Crosses it out. Returns to the longer, harder version.}
\action{No dialogue. The correction is the whole scene.}
